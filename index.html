      How this works

      Data: a GitHub Action refreshes data/sleeper.json (stats and projections) and data/league.json (your ESPN league) every 4 hours. If those files aren't there yet, the page pulls Sleeper data straight from your browser.

      Draft advisor score = points over a replacement-level player at that position, plus a bonus when the next-best player at that position is likely to be much worse by your next turn, times how much your roster needs that position.

      Weekly lineup: starts your highest-projected players. If you're the underdog it leans toward high-ceiling players; if you're the favorite it leans toward safe ones.

      What the colors mean

      Position tags are colored only so you can scan a list fast: QB RB WR TE K D/ST. The color says nothing about how good the player is.

      Badges do carry meaning:

      • green good: an Easy matchup, or trending (lots of people are adding him right now)
      • yellow caution: Questionable or Doubtful injury, or may be gone before your pick
      • red problem: Out or on IR, on a BYE, or a Tough matchup
      • gray neutral info, like an average matchup
